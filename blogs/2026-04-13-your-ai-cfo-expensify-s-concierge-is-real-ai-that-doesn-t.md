Definition What is an AI-enabled CFO? The term "AI CFO" is sometimes used to describe a software product, a futurist concept, or occasionally a job title nobody actually has yet. But the most useful definition is simpler: an AI-enabled or AI-powered CFO is a finance leader who uses automation, data intelligence, and integrated financial systems to make faster, better decisions.
